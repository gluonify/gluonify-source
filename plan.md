Not verified on a cluster. Not checked: an override set directly as `QUARKUS_OIDC_TOKEN_ISSUER` / `QUARKUS_OIDC_AUTH_SERVER_URL`, and `GLUONIFY_SOURCE_OIDC_REALM` contradicting the platform issuer without an issuer override.
